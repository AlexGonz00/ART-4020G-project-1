MECHANICAL KEYBOARD GUIDE

WHY Get A Mechanical Keyboard?

There are plenty of reasons a person would consider buying a mechanical keyboard:

  • Durability
  • Customizability/Aesthetics
  • Feel
  • Niche needs

Whatever the reason, this page will serve as a guide on the parts needed to assemble your own mechanical keyboard!

What makes up a keyboard?

All mechanical keyboards are composed of the following items:

  1. PCB
  2. Mechanical switches
  3. Stabilizers
  4. Case/Plate
  5. Keycaps

Keyboard Parts

Here's a quick breakdown on each of the mentioned parts:

PCB

The printed circuit board registers key presses and connects the keyboard's electronic components

Mechanical Switches

The switches sit beneath each keycap and register a key press when activated

Stabilizers

Stabilizers support larger keys, such as the spacebar and Shift key, to keep them balanced and prevent wobbling

Case/Plate

The case houses and protects the keyboard’s components, while the plate holds the switches securely in place

Keycaps

Keycaps are the removable tops of the keys that provide the surface you press and determine the keyboard’s appearance and feel
